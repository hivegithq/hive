Intent

Add a buy-two-get-one-free promotion: a new pure helper in src/promotion.ts computes, per SKU, the cents saved by making every third unit free (quantity 3+). checkout() applies the saving after the subtotal and before the discount, and reports it as a new `promotion` field on Receipt. Tests cover 2, 3 and 7 units (plus per-SKU isolation and stacking with a discount code); the existing receipt deepEqual test is updated for the new field.
